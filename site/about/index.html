FAQ

Common Corporate Travel Questions

What Is A Business Travel Agent?

“A business travel agent, also known as a corporate travel agent, provides a service-based solution for companies to outsource their business travel needs. Typically, these agents handle the entire life cycle of travel bookings from start to finish and offer additional services such as traveler risk management, fare procurement, reporting, invoicing, and more.”

What Does A Business Travel Agency Do?

“A business travel agency, also known as a corporate travel agency, provides companies with a variety of travel booking services including flights, rail, hotels, meetings, and events. These services are tailored to the needs of business travelers, helping them save time and money while ensuring their safety as a duty of care.”

What Is A Management Company {TMC}?

“A Travel Management Company (TMC) offers a wider range of comprehensive management services and end-to-end solutions. In addition to traditional booking services provided by business travel agents, a TMC takes a consultative approach to help companies manage their travel program and budget. Contact our team today to learn more about how our TMC services can benefit your business.”
